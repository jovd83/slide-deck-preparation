# Slide Archetypes & Templates

Use these archetypes to guide the rhetorical and visual layout of each slide. Every slide must follow the prescribed **Template** structure.

## Foundational
1. **The Big Thesis:** The overarching claim or opening statement.
   *Template:* [Headline Vision Statement] -> [3 Core Supporting Pillars] -> [The "So What?" for the audience]
2. **Problem / Solution:** Dual layout contrasting the pain point with the fix.
   *Template:* [The Pain Point] -> [Quantified Impact of inaction] -> [The Proposed Solution] -> [Immediate Benefit]
3. **Pro / Con:** Comparison of advantages and disadvantages.
   *Template:* [Balanced List of Benefits] vs [Acknowledged Drawbacks/Risks] -> [Final Recommendation]
4. **Three Reasons:** A triptych detailing three supporting pillars.
   *Template:* [Reason 1 + Proof] | [Reason 2 + Proof] | [Reason 3 + Proof]
5. **Risk / Mitigation:** Identifying a threat and how it will be managed.
   *Template:* [Identified Risk] -> [Probability/Impact] -> [Mitigation Strategy] -> [Residual Risk Level]
6. **Before / After:** The transformation or change over time.
   *Template:* [Current Inefficient State] -> [The Intervention] -> [Future Optimized State]
7. **Timeline / History:** Chronological sequence of events.
   *Template:* [Past Milestone] -> [Present Status] -> [Future Target]
8. **Quote / Testimonial:** Emphasis on a key voice or customer.
   *Template:* [The Quote] -> [Attribution/Source] -> [The Context/Why it matters]
9. **Data Spotlight:** A single, massive number driving a specific point.
   *Template:* [One Key Metric] -> [The Source] -> [Comparison/Benchmark] -> [What this proves]
10. **The Process:** Step-by-step instructions.
    *Template:* [Step 1: Input] -> [Step 2: Action] -> [Step 3: Outcome] -> [Step 4: Next Step]

## Analytical
11. **Current State / Future State:** Gap analysis showing the transition.
    *Template:* [Snapshot of Now] -> [The Obstacle] -> [Snapshot of the Future] -> [The Path Between]
12. **Case Study:** Context, Action, Result (CAR) format.
    *Template:* [The Client/Context] -> [The Specific Challenge] -> [The Deployed Solution] -> [Measured Result]
13. **Anatomy of X:** Exploded view or breakdown of a single concept.
    *Template:* [Component A] | [Component B] | [Component C] -> [How they integrate together]
14. **Myth vs. Fact:** Debunking common misconceptions.
    *Template:* [Common Myth] -> [Why people believe it] -> [The Fact/Reality] -> [Evidence]
15. **Frequently Asked Question:** Q&A format addressing objections.
    *Template:* [The Question/Objection] -> [The Concise Answer] -> [Supporting Data]
16. **The Funnel:** Top-to-bottom process (e.g., sales or conversion).
    *Template:* [Awareness Stage] -> [Consideration Stage] -> [Decision Stage] -> [Conversion Goal]
17. **The Flywheel:** Cyclical, self-reinforcing process.
    *Template:* [Input Action] -> [Growth Trigger] -> [Network Effect] -> [Return to Start]
18. **Venn Diagram:** Intersection of multiple concepts.
    *Template:* [Concept A] | [Concept B] | [Concept C] -> [The Sweet Spot in the Center]
19. **The Matrix:** 2x2 grid mapping items across two axes.
    *Template:* [Axis X Label] | [Axis Y Label] -> [Quadrant Breakdown] -> [Where we sit]
20. **The Iceberg:** What is visible above the surface vs. underlying causes.
    *Template:* [Visible Symptom] -> [Underlying Cause 1] -> [Underlying Cause 2] -> [Root Systemic Issue]

## Strategic
21. **Cost / Benefit:** ROI focus, investment versus return.
    *Template:* [Initial Investment Cost] -> [Operational Costs] vs [Quantified Savings] -> [Payback Period]
22. **Deep Dive:** Magnifying a very specific, granular aspect.
    *Template:* [The Macro Context] -> [Zoom into Variable X] -> [Granular Data] -> [Insight]
23. **The Pivot:** Explaining a sudden change in direction or strategy.
    *Template:* [Original Strategy] -> [Market/Data Shift] -> [The New Direction] -> [Why it wins]
24. **Key Stakeholders:** Who is involved and what their roles are.
    *Template:* [Internal Champions] | [Decision Makers] | [End Users] -> [Approval Path]
25. **Market Landscape:** Competitor mapping and positioning.
    *Template:* [Competitor Set] -> [Differentiator Axis] -> [Our Unique Value Proposition]
26. **SWOT Analysis:** Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats.
    *Template:* [Internal Strengths] | [Internal Weaknesses] | [External Opportunities] | [External Threats]
27. **The Catalyst:** The trigger event forcing action now.
    *Template:* [Stable State] -> [The Shock/Trigger] -> [The Crisis/Opportunity] -> [Immediate Required Action]
28. **Day in the Life:** Persona journey or user experience walkthrough.
    *Template:* [Morning: The Struggle] -> [Midday: The Solution Interaction] -> [Evening: The Success]
29. **The Architecture:** System design or organizational structure.
    *Template:* [Frontend/User Layer] -> [Logic/Business Layer] -> [Data/Infrastructure Layer]
30. **The Paradox:** Highlighting conflicting truths that must be balanced.
    *Template:* [Truth A] vs [Opposing Truth B] -> [The Synthesis/Balance Point]

## Persuasive & Visionary
31. **The North Star:** The guiding metric or ultimate vision.
    *Template:* [The Single Metric] -> [How it aligns the team] -> [Long-term Impact]
32. **The Moat:** The competitive advantage or barrier to entry.
    *Template:* [Threat] -> [Our Barrier (IP, Network, Brand)] -> [Sustainability of Advantage]
33. **Unintended Consequences:** Ripple effects or secondary impacts.
    *Template:* [Primary Action] -> [Secondary Effect A] -> [Tertiary Effect B (The 'Gotcha')]
34. **The Antipattern:** What NOT to do (examples of failure).
    *Template:* [Common Failure Path] -> [Why it looks attractive] -> [Why it fails] -> [The Better Way]
35. **The Silver Bullet:** Addressing the desire for an easy fix.
    *Template:* [The Desired "Easy Fix"] -> [Why it is a distraction] -> [The Real, Hard Solution]
36. **Feature vs. Benefit:** Translating what a thing is into why it matters.
    *Template:* [Technical Feature] -> [User Capability] -> [Emotional/Business Benefit]
37. **The Roadmap:** Gantt chart or milestone view of the future.
    *Template:* [Q1 Milestone] -> [Q2 Milestone] -> [Q3 Milestone] -> [The Destination]
38. **The Bottleneck:** Constraint analysis showing what is holding things back.
    *Template:* [Full System Flow] -> [The One Point of Friction] -> [Impact on Throughput] -> [Solution]
39. **The Root Cause:** The "5 Whys" digging into the real issue.
    *Template:* [Observation] -> [Why? (Level 1)] -> [Why? (Level 2)] -> [The Core Root Cause]
40. **The Spectrum:** A range of options across a continuum.
    *Template:* [Conservative Extreme] <---- [The Middle Way] ----> [Aggressive Extreme]

## Conceptual & Closing
41. **The Hierarchy:** Maslow-style pyramid of needs or priorities.
    *Template:* [Base Level (Mandatory)] -> [Mid-Level] -> [Top Level (Aspirational)]
42. **The Ecosystem:** How interconnected parts relate to the whole.
    *Template:* [The Central Hub] -> [Surrounding Nodes] -> [The Flow of Value]
43. **The Long Tail:** Distribution showing the head vs. the tail.
    *Template:* [The 20% Drivers] vs [The 80% Niche Opportunities] -> [Focus Strategy]
44. **The Checklist:** Requirements or prerequisites to move forward.
    *Template:* [Prerequisite 1] | [Prerequisite 2] | [Prerequisite 3] -> [Ready to Launch?]
45. **The Scorecard:** KPI tracking or grading system.
    *Template:* [Metric A: Grade] | [Metric B: Grade] | [Metric C: Grade] -> [Overall Performance]
46. **The Trade-off:** Acknowledging what must be sacrificed.
    *Template:* [Choice A: Speed] vs [Choice B: Quality] -> [Why we chose X]
47. **The Elephant in the Room:** Directly addressing the unspoken truth.
    *Template:* [The Obvious Concern] -> [Why we are avoiding it] -> [The Direct Answer]
48. **The Call to Action:** Explicit next steps for the audience.
    *Template:* [Immediate Step 1] -> [Immediate Step 2] -> [Point of Contact]
49. **The Summary:** TL;DR recap.
    *Template:* [Point A] | [Point B] | [Point C] -> [Final Closing Thought]
50. **The Appendix:** Deep reference material.
    *Template:* [Raw Data Table] | [Full Methodology] | [Link to full report]
51. **The Glossary:** Definition of key terms.
    *Template:* [Term 1: Definition] | [Term 2: Definition]
52. **The Visionary:** Inspirational, emotive close.
    *Template:* [The Emotional Peak] -> [The Call to Greatness] -> [Final Image/Quote]
53. **The Post-mortem:** Lessons learned from a past endeavor.
    *Template:* [What went well] -> [What failed] -> [Key Lesson for Next Time]
